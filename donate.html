FAQs

👨‍🎓

Why support music education?

Donating to music education for students empowers young minds by nurturing creativity and enhancing cognitive development. It provides students with opportunities to express themselves and build self-confidence, critical skills for success in life. Supporting music education also helps preserve cultural heritage and promotes a more vibrant and diverse society.

❤️

Where does the money go?

Your generous support enables us to continue our vital work, which includes: Purchasing musical instruments and equipment for our students and classrooms. Supporting community outreach programs to inspire and engage local youth through music.

🎶

Can I donate items other than money?

The Yudah School of Music is grateful for donations of food and snacks for performances, performance clothing, lessons, and instruments to support refugees in need. Contact us to make donations. Your generosity will make a direct and positive impact on the lives of those we serve.
