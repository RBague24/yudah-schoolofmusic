Our Story

Building equitable access to music education in Delaware

Our Foundation

A Mission Born From Passion

The Yudah School of Music was founded in 2025 by Rasháwn King, a 25-year-old educator dedicated to transforming lives through music. Recognizing the inequitable access to quality music education in Delaware, King established Yudah to serve approximately 50 students—primarily Black and Latino elementary and middle schoolers from underserved communities in Wilmington and Dover.

Every student receives completely free tuition, removing financial barriers and ensuring that background and socioeconomic status never determine a child's access to exceptional musical training.

Core Philosophy

At Yudah, we believe anyone can love music. We use music as a unifier within the community, breaking down barriers and creating pathways for young people to discover their potential, express themselves, and build confidence.

Our Programs

Comprehensive Music Education

Primary Focus: Yudah specializes in strings and piano instruction, with classes held at Solid Rock Baptist Church in Dover and Union American Methodist Episcopal in Wilmington. Rasháwn King personally teaches most lessons, ensuring personalized attention and mentorship.

Classical & Cultural Training

Beyond classical music fundamentals, Yudah incorporates Hispanic musical traditions including salsa and cumbia, connecting students to their cultural heritage while building musical excellence.

Landmark Performances: The school recently presented Handel's Messiah in December 2025 using period instruments and historically informed performance practices—a groundbreaking achievement featuring Baroque bows and authentic A=415 pitch. This performance showcased not only the musicianship of our students but also their deep engagement with classical traditions.

Creating Impact

Community-Centered Approach

Yudah operates on a lean budget of approximately $60,000+ annually, sustained through grants, fundraising, and generous donations from supporters. Despite limited resources, the organization has created something transformative for its students and their families.

Making Music Accessible

The school offers free public concerts to eliminate financial barriers for families. This commitment ensures that quality music experiences are available to everyone in the community, regardless of their ability to pay.

Every decision at Yudah is guided by the belief that music education should be a right, not a privilege. By breaking down barriers—financial, cultural, and social—we're creating pathways for young people to unlock their potential and discover their voices.
