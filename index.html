Our Mission

At The Yudah School of Music, we are dedicated to empowering historically marginalized youth by creating a welcoming and nurturing environment where they can flourish as both disciplined individuals and accomplished musicians.

Our mission is to provide transformative cultural and musical opportunities, focusing on our commitment to inclusivity and excellence. We believe that every child deserves access to advanced musical training, regardless of race, ethnicity, creed, or socio-economic status, allowing them to unlock their fullest academic, behavioral, and cognitive potential. Join us in our journey to foster a brighter future for Delaware's youth.
